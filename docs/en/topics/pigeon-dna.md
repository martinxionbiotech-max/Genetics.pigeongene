# Racing Pigeon DNA

DNA is the carrier of genetic information in racing pigeons. Understanding the basic structure of DNA and marker technology is the first step to reading a DNA test report.

## Plain-language explanation (an analogy)

DNA is like the **"master manual for 40 chromosomes"** (the pigeon chromosome number is 2n=80, i.e. 40 pairs, referring here to a total of 40 pairs of chromosomes). Every pigeon is a unique "revised edition" of this manual, and the differences lie in subtle changes in the base sequence.

## DNA basics

- DNA is made up of four bases, A, T, C and G, and the sequence stores genetic information;
- A gene is a DNA segment that encodes a protein or performs a regulatory function;
- The pigeon genome is around 1.1 billion base pairs, and a reference genome has been assembled (Holt 2018, G3);
- Sequence differences (variation) between individuals are the source of genetic diversity.

## Common marker types

- **SNP (single nucleotide polymorphism)**: variation at a single base position, the most numerous type, suited to high-throughput typing;
- **Microsatellite (STR)**: differences in the number of repeats of a short sequence, mostly used for individual identification and parentage analysis;
- **Indel**: small insertions or deletions.

## How testing works

DNA testing amplifies target fragments by PCR and reads the genotype at a locus. qPCR is used for pathogen detection, while SNP/STR typing is used for gene and identity testing. The "genotype" in a report describes the combination of alleles an individual carries at a particular locus.

## Three major applications of DNA testing

- **Gene testing**: candidate performance gene genotyping, a breeding reference;
- **DNA identity card**: unique markers for an individual, pedigree management;
- **Parentage verification**: STR locus comparison to verify parent-offspring relationships.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does DNA testing hurt the pigeon?**

No. Sampling uses feathers, blood cards or buccal swabs, which are minimally invasive procedures.

**Can a DNA identity card prevent cheating?**

DNA markers cannot be forged, so they can serve as a basis for verifying the identity of an individual.

**Is pigeon DNA like human DNA?**

It is the same DNA-based inheritance system, but the sequences and genome structures differ enormously.
