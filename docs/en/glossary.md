# Glossary

| Term | English | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| DNA | Deoxyribonucleic Acid | Deoxyribonucleic acid, the carrier of genetic information: a double-helix molecule built from the four bases A, T, C and G. |
| Gene | Gene | A segment of DNA that encodes a functional product (usually a protein); the basic unit of inheritance. |
| Allele | Allele | A different version of a gene at the same locus; an individual carries two alleles at each locus. |
| Genotype | Genotype | The combination of alleles an individual carries at a particular gene locus. |
| Phenotype | Phenotype | The observable characteristics an individual shows, determined by genotype and environment together. |
| Genetic Marker | Genetic Marker | A variant site in the genome that can be detected, used for genotyping, individual identification and relationship analysis. |
| STR | Short Tandem Repeat | A short tandem repeat sequence whose repeat number varies greatly between individuals, used to build DNA fingerprints. |
| SNP | Single Nucleotide Polymorphism | A variation at a single base in the genome; an important genetic basis for differences in traits. |
| PCR | Polymerase Chain Reaction | A technique for amplifying a specific DNA fragment in vitro. |
| qPCR | Real-time Quantitative PCR | Real-time fluorescence quantitative PCR, a technique that quantifies a target nucleic acid in real time through a fluorescent signal. |
| Genome | Genome | The sum of all the genetic information of an organism. |
| Chromosome | Chromosome | The carrier of genetic material, made up of DNA and protein. |
| Quantitative Trait | Quantitative Trait | A trait influenced by many genes and by the environment together, showing a continuous distribution. |
| Heritability | Heritability | The proportion of phenotypic variation explained by genetic factors. |
| Polymorphism | Polymorphism | The phenomenon of several alleles existing at the same gene locus within a population. |
| Dominant | Dominant | An allele whose trait is expressed even in the heterozygous state. |
| Recessive | Recessive | An allele whose trait is expressed only in the homozygous state. |
| Parentage Testing | Parentage Testing | A method of confirming the parent-offspring relationship between individuals by analysing genetic markers. |
| DNA Fingerprint | DNA Fingerprint | An individual-specific genetic profile built from several genetic markers. |
| Genotyping | Genotyping | The process of detecting an individual's genotype at a particular gene locus. |
| Homozygote | Homozygote | An individual whose two alleles at a gene locus are identical (for example AA, aa). |
| Heterozygote | Heterozygote | An individual whose two alleles at a gene locus differ (for example Aa). |
| Mendelian Inheritance | Mendelian Inheritance | The inheritance rules revealed by Mendel, including the law of segregation and the law of independent assortment. |
| Candidate Gene | Candidate Gene | A gene that, on the basis of functional reasoning, may be related to a trait and needs further validation. |
| Epigenetics | Epigenetics | Heritable changes in the regulation of gene expression that do not alter the DNA sequence. |
| Genetic Marker | Genetic Marker | A variant site in the genome that can be detected, used for genotyping and individual identification. |
