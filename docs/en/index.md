# Racing Pigeon Genetics Library

The PigeonGene Genetics Library (genetics.senopigeon.com) is the genetics backbone of the PigeonGene digital ecosystem. It provides **scientific and carefully worded** racing pigeon genetics knowledge for fanciers and breeders.

## What you will find here

- [Gene Encyclopedia](genes/index.md): the function, research evidence and interpretation limits of eight performance-related gene loci (LDHA, DRD4, CRY1, MSTN, LRP8, GSR, CASK, F-KER). Each page states its evidence grade and its verified literature sources.
- [Genetics topics](topics/index.md): guided reading across 12 topics, including breeding genetics, performance genetics, homing and endurance genetics, inbreeding coefficient and plumage colour genetics.
- [Genetic markers](genetic-markers.md): marker types such as STR and SNP, the genotyping workflow, and their limitations.
- [Glossary](glossary.md): core genetics terms explained.
- [FAQ](faq.md): common questions about the limits of genetic testing and how to read a DNA report.

## Who this is for

- **Fanciers**: understand what "may be associated" really means in a DNA report, and avoid over-interpretation;
- **Breeders**: see the genetics behind inbreeding coefficient, heritability and pairing strategy;
- **Researchers**: trace every literature source cited here (PMID/DOI verifiable).

## Scientific statement

> Gene associations are **probabilistic and exploratory** information. Wording throughout this site is limited to "may be associated / research suggests", and does **not** constitute a prediction or guarantee of race performance. Actual racing performance results from genes, training, nutrition, health and environment acting together.
